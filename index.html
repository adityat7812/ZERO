Strategy

How to actually clear a backlog

The plan gets you the numbers. These rules get you to the finish line.

Rule 01

Never break the chain twice

Missing one day is an accident. Missing two is a new habit. If you skip a day, the very next day is non-negotiable — even if you only do half the target.

Rule 02

Finish one subject before starting another

Rotating between five half-finished subjects feels productive but produces nothing. Pick the scariest backlog first and clear it fully.

Rule 03

Watch at 1.5×, not 2×

2× saves time but destroys retention, so you pay it back twice in revision. 1.5× is the sweet spot for most NEET aspirants.

Rule 04

Notes during, not after

Pausing to write detailed notes is where most backlogs grow. Write short pointers during the lecture, then spend 10 minutes consolidating after.

Rule 05

Protect one revision day per week

Clearing lectures you've forgotten isn't clearing a backlog. Keep one day purely for revision of what you already finished.

Rule 06

Solve questions the same day

Every lecture should be followed by at least 10–15 MCQs. Without questions, a lecture is just entertainment.

Rule 07

Re-plan every Sunday

Life happens. Open this planner every week, update your hours, and regenerate. A plan you adjust weekly beats a perfect plan you abandoned.

Rule 08

Track hours, not lectures

Some lectures are 90 minutes of pain, others are 30 minutes of ease. Hours are the only honest unit of progress.

Rule 09

Use the timer, every session

Starting is the hardest part. A 25-minute Pomodoro is easy to start. Once you're in, momentum carries you forward.

Rule 10

Never study past your limit

If your capacity is 6 hours, don't push to 10. The 4 extra hours come out of tomorrow's focus. Consistency beats intensity.

Frequently asked questions

How does backlog.com calculate my hours? +
It multiplies your lecture count by average duration, divides by your playback speed, then adds your chosen overhead % for notes and revision. That's your effective watch time — the honest number, not the raw hours.
Is my data saved anywhere? +
Only in your own browser via localStorage. Nothing leaves your device. You can export a backup JSON from the Backlog page at any time.
What if my deadline is impossible? +
The planner will tell you exactly how many hours a day you'd need. You then have three options: extend the deadline, increase daily hours, or drop/shorten some topics. It's an honest reality check, not a judgement.
How does the streak work? +
Complete at least one Pomodoro focus session or tick one day on your plan and your streak increments. Miss a full day and it resets. Best streak is saved separately.
Can I print my plan? +
Yes — click Print on the Plan page. It renders a clean black-and-white version with just your stat cards and schedule.
Does it work on mobile? +
Yes. The whole app is responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops. Use the menu button on the top right on mobile.
